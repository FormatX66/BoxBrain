AURUM TINY SEED — PHYSICAL HANDOFF

This packet is consumed only after CI has produced AURUM-TINYSEED-RELEASE.json with state READY_TO_FLASH.

Required order on each physical target:
1. Verify the artifact SHA-256 against AURUM-TINYSEED-RELEASE.json.
2. Flash only the explicitly selected non-system target media.
3. Read back or otherwise verify the written media before booting it.
4. Boot the target from Tiny Seed.
5. Require the boot console/journal to contain both:
   AURUM_TINYSEED_READY
   AURUM_BOOT_PROOF_READY
6. Preserve /var/lib/aurum/evidence/boot-proof.json when the root is persistent, or capture the console marker on live media.
7. Confirm Guardian state shows a valid A/B schema with an LKG slot before any regrow operation.
8. Allow reseed/regrow to build only the inactive slot. Never overwrite LKG.
9. Reboot into the armed trial and require Guardian health evidence before promotion.
10. After a healthy promotion is proven, run the controlled rollback drill on a disposable candidate and require automatic return to LKG plus quarantine evidence.

Do not call physical boot proven from a VM smoke test. Do not call rollback proven from a unit test. Do not flash if the release manifest is missing, hashes disagree, or the target disk cannot be identified unambiguously.

Evidence expected after the physical gates:
- boot-proof.json or captured AURUM_BOOT_PROOF_READY receipt
- Guardian slots.json before trial
- Guardian slots.json after healthy promotion
- Guardian slots.json after forced bad-candidate rollback
- quarantine record naming the failed candidate/reason

When these receipts exist, the seed can advance from READY_TO_FLASH to PHYSICALLY_BOOTED and RECOVERY_PROVEN.
