# Aurum Brand Identity

## Approved mark

Aurum's approved visual direction is the **Au + seven-leaf growth mark**: the original woven/codelation-inspired `Au` identity remains the core, with a restrained golden branch growing from the mark.

The current approved raster reference is:

`Projects/Aurum/assets/brand/aurum-seven-leaf-mark-approved.png`

## Meaning

- **Au** preserves the original Aurum identity and the gold/aurum correlation.
- **Gold** is the dominant human-visible identity color.
- **Teal** carries the machine-state/codelation accent.
- **The seed/node** is the point of origin.
- **Seven leaves** express unfolding growth and possibility without imposing seven fixed capabilities or traits.
- **The branch grows from the mark** rather than decorating around it: Aurum develops from a stable semantic core.

The seven leaves are visual language, not a rigid architectural count. Aurum can gain any number of traits/TR8s while preserving the same brand mark.

## Brand language

Primary growth line:

> **Plant the seed. Aurum grows from there.**

Supporting line used with the seven-leaf presentation:

> **One seed. Endless possibilities.**

Both express the same idea: the seed is stable, while Aurum adapts and grows to help the person and the machine it inhabits.

## Production rules

1. Keep the `Au` mark dominant; growth must never overwhelm it.
2. Keep the branch asymmetrical, organic, and restrained. Avoid radial lotus/mandala geometry or religious visual language.
3. Preserve seven visible leaves in the primary growth mark.
4. Maintain gold + teal as the core palette, with dark/black presentation preferred where appropriate.
5. Simplified icons may remove small detail but must remain recognizably the same `Au + growth` identity.
6. The earlier first selected Au raster remains preserved as ancestry and geometry reference; it is not discarded by this evolution.
7. Future boot animation may express the same identity as **seed → Au → growth/bloom**, provided the static mark remains recognizable.

## Architectural fit

The identity mirrors Aurum's generational architecture:

**stable semantic seed → machine-specific growth → inherited lineage**

The user does not have to understand that architecture to recognize the brand. The visual message is simply that Aurum starts small, adapts, helps, and grows.
